Version: 0.0.1
Date: 2026-09-19
  Major Features:
    - Initial release: standalone train dispatcher with providers, requesters, depots, fuel, cleanup and manager in one mod.
  Features:
    - UTL train stop with built-in logic, or UTL station combinator wired to a normal train stop.
    - Roles: provider, requester, depot, fuel station, cleanup; named networks.
    - Requests in the station window as target stock; values for train length, max. trains, thresholds, priorities and locked slots.
    - Items and fluids, several goods per train, next job right away after unloading.
    - Your schedule, train groups and interrupts are kept (UTL only adds temporary stops).
    - Automatic refueling and cleanup; cleanup stations accept all items, all fluids or chosen goods.
    - Depots with train length, trains move to a fitting depot by themselves.
    - UTL Manager: depots, stations, inventory, history and alerts.
    - Alerts that can be turned off per player.
    - Copy settings with Shift-click; blueprints keep the settings.
    - Tips & tricks with example scenes and a large example scenario.
    - German and English.
